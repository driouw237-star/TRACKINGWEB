    Customs & documentation

    Every international shipment needs a commercial invoice and a packing list. Our licensed brokers handle HS classification, duty calculation, and regulatory filings at origin and destination — so you don't have to.

    Commercial invoiceRequired for all cross-border shipments
    Packing listRequired for shipments over 3 pieces
    Certificate of originRequired for certain goods and regions
    Duty & taxPaid by receiver unless DDP selected

    Packaging guidelines

    • Use double-walled boxes for fragile or heavy items
    • Fill void space with bubble wrap or paper — no movement inside
    • Seal all seams with reinforced packing tape
    • Label each piece with the tracking code and receiver address
